Public intake form

Public Records Request

Submit a request under the Colorado Open Records Act. Fields marked * are required.

Before you begin

Describe the records as specifically as possible, including dates, locations, departments, custodians, or document names when known. Vague, broad, or voluminous requests may take additional time.

Records are typically made available within three working days when available under C.R.S. § 24-72-201 et seq. Extenuating circumstances may extend the response period by seven additional working days.

Requester information

Requested record information

Fees and delivery

Digital copies have no per-page charge. Research and retrieval time may be charged after the first hour at the County’s current published rate. Payment may be required before release, and a deposit may be required for voluminous requests.

Request for Child Welfare Records

Request records under C.R.S. § 19-1-307. Fields marked * are required.

Confidentiality notice

Reports of child abuse or neglect are confidential and are not public information. A requester must establish an applicable right of access. Records may be heavily redacted to protect reporters and other individuals.

You must provide identification. Do not upload actual identification while testing this public prototype.
